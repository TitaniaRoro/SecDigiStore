FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a verified Business Manager account?

A verified Business Manager (BM) account is a Facebook Business Manager that has completed Facebook's business verification process. This gives your account higher trust levels, increased spending limits, and better access to advertising features.

How long does delivery take?

Most products are delivered instantly or within 1-2 hours after payment confirmation. You'll receive all account credentials via email immediately after purchase.

Is there a warranty on purchased accounts?

Yes! All products come with comprehensive warranty ranging from 30 to 90 days depending on the product. If you experience issues during the warranty period, we'll replace or refund your purchase.

Are these accounts safe for advertising?

Absolutely! All accounts are verified through official Facebook channels and are completely safe for advertising. They come with clean histories and are ready for immediate campaign deployment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards, cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, USDT), and bank transfers. Contact our sales team for custom payment arrangements.

Do you provide setup assistance?

Yes! Our support team provides free setup assistance to help you configure your new accounts. We also offer detailed documentation and video tutorials.

What is WABA verification?

WABA (WhatsApp Business API) verification is the official process to get your WhatsApp Business account verified with the green tick badge, enabling advanced features and building customer trust.

Can I transfer the account to my email?

Yes, all accounts can be transferred to your email address. We provide full ownership transfer with complete instructions.

What if I face issues after warranty period?

Even after the warranty period, our support team is available to help you with any issues. We offer extended support packages for long-term customers.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk orders. Contact our sales team to discuss your requirements and get a custom quote.
