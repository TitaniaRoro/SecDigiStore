Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Warranty Periods

Our products come with the following warranty periods:

  • Verified Business Manager: 90 days
  • BM with Ad Account: 60 days
  • WhatsApp Business API: 30 days
  • Facebook Pages: 30 days
  • Verification Services: Until verification complete

2. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a refund if:

  • The account is restricted or banned within the warranty period
  • The account does not match the product description
  • We are unable to deliver the purchased product
  • Technical issues prevent account access (within first 24 hours)

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will NOT be provided for:

  • Accounts restricted due to user violation of platform policies
  • Requests made after warranty expiration
  • Change of mind after account delivery
  • User negligence in securing account credentials
  • Advertising disapprovals or account limitations caused by user actions

4. Refund Process

To request a refund:

  1. Contact support within the warranty period
  2. Provide order details and issue description
  3. Our team will investigate within 24-48 hours
  4. If approved, refund processed within 3-5 business days

5. Replacement Option

In most cases, we offer account replacement instead of refunds. Replacements are provided free of charge within the warranty period.

6. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be offered in certain situations where only part of the service was affected or delivered.

7. Contact for Refunds

For refund requests, contact: support@verifiedbmandwaba.com
